questions you might be asking

who is lights out for? +

anyone whose phone is the last thing they touch at night and the reason they’re still awake. people doing revenge bedtime procrastination, doomscrollers, shift workers, and anyone who’s tried screen-time limits and tapped straight past them. it doubles as a daytime focus blocker too.

how is this different from a screen-time limit or opal? +

two things. first, the shield is built to actually hold at night, not to be dismissed in one tap. second, lights out is a bedtime app first: every curfew opens into a real wind-down, built on CBT-I, not just a blocked screen. and it’s fully offline, with no account.

what’s CBT-I? +

cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia, the first-line, evidence-based treatment for sleeplessness. lights out is built on its four pillars: stimulus control, a consistent wind-down, a kinder relationship with the bed, and gentle daytime structure. there’s a short learn section in the app for each.

is my data really only on my phone? +

yes. there is no lights out server. your schedules, your sleep logs, your journal, all of it lives in a local database on your phone. there’s no cloud because there is no cloud, and no analytics or crash reporting either. the only thing that leaves your phone is your purchase receipt, to the app store. that’s the entire list.

will there be iphone and android? +

yes, both at launch. the blocking uses each platform’s native screen-time controls, so it works the same on either.
